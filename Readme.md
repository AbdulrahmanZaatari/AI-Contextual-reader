# 📖 AI Contextual Reader

A smart reading assistant powered by AI that helps you understand PDFs better.

## Getting Started

**First Time?**
1. Visit the app link (you'll be sent this)
2. Click "Sign Up" and create a username and password
3. Remember your username and password - you'll use these every time

**Your Account**
- Your account is private - only you can see your books and notes
- Your progress is saved automatically - you'll resume where you left off

### How to Use

**1. Upload a PDF**
- Click "Upload" in the left sidebar
- Select any PDF from your computer
- It will appear in your book list

**2. Read**
- Click the book name to open it
- Use Previous/Next buttons or jump to a specific page
- The app remembers which page you were on

**3. Analyze Text**
- Copy any text from the page
- Paste it into the "Paste text:" box
- Choose an action:
  - **Translate** - Translate to English
  - **Explain** - Get a clear explanation
  - **ELI5** - Simple explanation
  - **Historical** - Learn the context
  - **Summary** - Quick summary
  - **Compare** - See connections to other ideas
  - **Themes** - Understand main themes
  - **Cite** - Get citation-worthy insights
  - **Ask** - Ask a custom question about the text

**4. Organize**
- **Bookmarks**: Mark important pages (click 🔖 button)
- **History**: See all your previous questions and answers
- **Export**: Download your notes as PDF or JSON

## Features

✅ **Your Own Books** - Upload as many PDFs as you want  
✅ **Save Progress** - Automatically remembers your reading position  
✅ **AI Analysis** - Ask questions about any text  
✅ **Organize** - Bookmark pages and view your full history  
✅ **Export** - Download all your work  
✅ **Secure** - Your data is private to your account  

## Tips

- The app works best with clearly scanned PDFs
- Longer texts get better AI explanations
- You can ask follow-up questions about the same text
- Bookmarks help you find important sections quickly
- Export your history regularly as backup

## Support

If something isn't working:
1. Try refreshing the page
2. Log out and log back in
3. Make sure you're using the correct username/password
